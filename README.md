💰 Bachat Buddy

Your Smart Companion for Saving Money and Managing Expenses.

Bachat Buddy is a personal finance management web application designed to help users manage their money, track expenses, and build better saving habits. It aims to make everyday money management simple, accessible, and user-friendly.

🌐 Live Demo:https://bachat-buddy.base44.app/
✨ Key Features

Expense Management – Keep track of your daily spending.
Savings Management – Monitor your savings and financial goals.
Budget Planning – Manage your budget and spending habits.
User-Friendly Interface – Simple and intuitive design.
Financial Awareness – Understand spending patterns and make smarter financial decisions.

🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of Bachat Buddy is to encourage smarter financial habits by helping people understand where their money goes and how they can save more effectively.
